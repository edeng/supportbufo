Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: December 4, 2024

Local Game Data

All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. To delete this data:

  • Uninstall the Keep Bufo Alive app from your device
  • Reinstall the app for a fresh start (optional)

Advertising Data

Keep Bufo Alive uses optional advertising services. You can control ad tracking in your device settings:

iOS Devices

  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Find "Keep Bufo Alive" in the list
  • Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

Android Devices

  • Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads
  • Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset your advertising ID if desired

Advertising Partners Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data collected by our advertising partners:

Unity Ads

  • Email: DPO@unity3d.com
  • Include your Advertising ID in the request
  • Specify that you're requesting data deletion for Keep Bufo Alive

Google AdMob

  • Visit Google Privacy Settings
  • Sign in to your Google Account
  • Navigate to "Ad personalization"
  • Turn off ad personalization or delete your advertising data

Note: Advertising is completely optional in Keep Bufo Alive. You can play the game without enabling any ad tracking.
